## Logbook

Underway for Da Nang. Anchor in Da Nang Harbor. 1230 — Bill Ducy (USMC) comes aboard ship. 1630 — Bill departs. .50 cal. machine gun watch. Observed large amount of air traffic. Set shore bomb exercise stations.
