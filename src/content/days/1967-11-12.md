## Logbook

Mike visited Bill at Camp Pendleton on a weekend liberty. The Jouett went to sea for a special operation — part of a task force escorting the USS Enterprise during a presidential visit to the West Coast.

They went to the zoo and Balboa Park. Mike warned Bill about sidewalk photographers in San Diego — *"they are all out to take your money"* — a hustler had already charged Bill $5.25 for a 5×7 black-and-white.

He was taking fire control courses and signing up for electronics correspondence courses that counted for junior college credit ($5 for the first course, the rest free, and you kept the textbooks).

## Sources

> "Bill was waiting on the pier when we got in. I brought him on the ship and we ate dinner then went into San Diego and spent the rest of the weekend there." [Letter]

> "We will be in port for 18 straight days before we go out again. We go out that time then will be here till the middle of January when we leave for West Pac." [Letter]
