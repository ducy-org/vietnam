## Logbook

After three weeks at Treasure Island, Mike received his assignment.

His research:

That weekend, Mike and his Great Lakes buddy crossed the bay to see the carrier USS Enterprise, freshly back from Vietnam:

He was considering buying an Instamatic camera ($14.50 at the Exchange). David's birthday was that day — Mike had mailed a card Saturday. First pay at Treasure Island: $92.00 for twenty days.

## Sources

> "This morning I was put on assingment. I got the USS JOUETT (DLG-29) out of San Diego. DLG stands for guided missle frigate. I went to the library and found out a little about it." [Letter]

> "So, it sounds like a good ship, especially since its not even a year old yet. The living conditions will probably be pretty good." [Letter]

> "Its neuclear powered and the biggest ship in the Navy. Let me tell you it is really big. It doesn't seem like your on a ship when you walk around on it." [Letter]
