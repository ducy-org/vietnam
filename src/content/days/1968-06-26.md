## Logbook

At sea, SAR station. 1800 — MIG-21 DOWNED. Mike's logbook credits the kill to USS Bon Homme Richard. The 1968 Command Operations Report tells it differently — crediting Jouett directly: "Richard TANNER, RD1, one of JOUETT's many expert AIC's (Air Intercept Controller) executed an intercept that resulted in the destruction of one MIG-21." Both are true: CDR Lowell "Moose" Myers (VF-51, F-8H Crusader, Bon Homme Richard) pulled the trigger — maneuvering behind the MiG, firing guns, then destroying it with an AIM-9 Sidewinder — but RD1 Tanner vectored the intercept from Jouett's CIC. Tanner received the Navy Commendation Medal. This was the Navy's 28th MiG kill. 2030 — Refuel from USS Chipola (AO-63).
