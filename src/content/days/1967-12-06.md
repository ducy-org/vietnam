## Logbook

Mike picked up his airline tickets and decided to wait at the airport for Bill's flight.

They spent the evening together at the USO before Bill caught the 9:00 bus back to Camp Pendleton. Mike bought Bill a wallet for his birthday — *"He had been using an old one of mine that I let him have when I saw how ragged and beat-up his was."*

Bill owed Mike $45. They stored their civilian clothes together in a locker on the ship.

The Jouett participated in "Blue Lotus," the largest fleet exercise in three years, with 44 ships and Royal Canadian Navy units. The ship celebrated her first birthday on December 3 at sea. [Familygram, 10 Dec 1967]

A Dependents' Cruise was held December 19, with over 100 wives and teenage children aboard. A Christmas party followed — Santa Claus and over 1,000 cookies baked by Jouett's cooks. A 60-foot tree of lights was erected on the foremast. [Familygram, 19 Jan 1968]

Mike flew home to Decatur on American Airlines for two weeks of Christmas leave. He returned to San Diego around January 2, 1968.
