## Logbook

Mike sent home his first roll of film from the ship. A $17 check arrived out of the blue — reimbursement for travel expenses from San Diego to Seattle. He was weighing whether to come home for Christmas.

He needed his "Military Requirements PO 3 and 2" book sent from home — the reserve center had failed to record that he'd completed the course, and he might have to retake the test in February.

## Sources

> "I guess the one big thing on my mind now is weather to come home for Christmas or not. I'll have to talk to Bill and see what he is going to do then maybe make up my mind." [Letter]
