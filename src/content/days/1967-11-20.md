## Logbook

The biggest news: Mike had made third class.

Christmas leave confirmed — starting December 19th, about 14 days. Pay jumped from $65 to $84 per payday.

Danny Wiggins — a friend from back home — was getting married. *"He ought to stay in the Army for another six years, they could really use his kind. ha ha"*

## Sources

> "If you noticed the return address, I am now third class. I found out last Wed. and sewed the 'crow' on today. I figure I have about $123 in back pay coming... It really feels great. Its like getting your report card and finding out you passed to the next grade. Now, I've got a chance to make second before I get out." [Letter]

> "We have one more operation to go out on. This one starts next Monday and lasts for about 5 days. We'll be here in port till the 16 of January when we go on the operation that really counts." [Letter]
