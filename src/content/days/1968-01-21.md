## Logbook

Arrive Hawaiian Islands. Shore bomb shoot at Kahoolawe Island. 1800 — Arrive and moor Pearl Harbor. Pay Day #62. Liberty on base, then into Honolulu, Hotel Street. Received mail for first time since leaving states.
