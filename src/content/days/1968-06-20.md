## Sources

> Meanwhile on Market Time — Given's letter, 20 June 1968: Mike's FTG "A" school classmate W.F. "Giv" Given (FTG-3, SN 7942408) wrote from a DER (radar picket escort ship) on Operation Market Time patrol. His ship served as mother ship to 4 Swift boats, carrying ammo, fuel, spare parts, and spare crews: "Once each day, each boat, comes alongside for a spare crew and re-supply." He'd been doing river supply runs and had no laundry or fresh water showers since Taiwan. Turning 20 and celebrating his 1st anniversary, Given was getting orders to USS Tulare (AKA-112) out of San Diego in September — a ship with 6x 3"/50 gun mounts relevant to his FTG rate. He planned to be in Subic July 3–25, then Guam for 5 weeks: "Just think Duc — pretty soon we're half way." [Given's letter to Mike]
