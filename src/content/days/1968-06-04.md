## Logbook

At sea, SAR station. Weather: clear, hot (air 85°, water 79°, wind 5 knots, calm seas, visibility 12 miles). 1300 — SAR ALERT: Aircraft downed 13 NM from Jouett. Location: 32mi 80° from Vinh, 24mi offshore, 15mi SE of Hon Matt Island. BIG MOTHER launched. RECOVERS ONE OF TWO CREW — injured, treated, evacuated to carrier for further aid. Resume search but UNABLE TO FIND OTHER CREW MEMBER. 1400 — USS Buckley (DD-808) boat brings debris and wreckage aboard Jouett. Mike records the aircraft as "A-5" in his logbook — information passed second-hand in the heat of the moment.
