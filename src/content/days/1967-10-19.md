## Logbook

The Jouett arrived a day early. Mike's first time at sea had been rough:

He was standing watches in the gun director:

The ship was now cruising at Condition III (wartime) watches — four hours on, four or eight off, plus regular work. Departure for WestPac confirmed: **January 16th.**

Seeing the wives and families on the pier when the ship returned from three months away:

## Sources

> "The first two weekends out I got seasick and the same thing happened last Saturday after we went out. But, I can assure you that I wasn't the only one who did. About 50% of the crew gets sick when we go out." [Letter]

> "There are three of us in there and it is like the inside of an Army tank." [Letter]

> "I can imagine what it will be like when we get back from the Wes Pac cruise." [Letter]
