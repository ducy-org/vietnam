## Logbook

At sea, SAR station. 0300 — SAR ALERT: A-7 Corsair (from USS America) downed 1000 yards inland, 20 miles from Jouett. Pilot calls he is OK with broken arm. SAR helo launched — unable to find pilot. 0600 — BIG MOTHER 71 takes part in search. 1050 — Big Mother 71 returns to ship WITH SMALL ARMS FIRE DAMAGE AND WOUNDED CREW MEMBER. 1200 — Send injured man back to USS Yorktown (CVS-10). DISCONTINUE SEARCH FOR PILOT — BELIEVED CAPTURED. 1900 — Rendezvous with USS Yorktown to attempt transfer of Big Mother 73 by crane. 2400 — Fail to transfer helo, return to South SAR station.
