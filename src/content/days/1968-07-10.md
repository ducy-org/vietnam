## Logbook

Underway for Pearl Harbor. 0850 — King Neptune and court board Jouett. 0900 — SHIP CROSSES EQUATOR AT 144th MERIDIAN. SHELLBACK INITIATION CEREMONY BEGINS. 1235 — Neptune Rex and court depart Jouett.

Underway for Pearl Harbor. 1100 — Approach Japanese fishing vessel, then resume course. (Crossed Date Line — same calendar date repeated.)
