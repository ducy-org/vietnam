## Logbook

At sea, SAR station. 1400 — SAR ALERT: F-4B Phantom (BuNo 151485, "Silver Kite NG-210") from VF-92 "Silver Kings," USS Enterprise, hit by MiG-21 Atoll air-to-air missile at 8,000 feet. Location: 18mi 40° from Vinh, 9mi offshore, 7mi NW of Hon Matt Island. Both crew eject 5 miles offshore. BOTH PILOTS RESCUED — LCDR Ejnar Christenson and LTJG Worth Kramer — by "Big Mother 71" (SH-3A Sea King, HC-7 Det 110). Crew: LTJG Richard A. Wiley (pilot), ENS Richard A. Everett (copilot), ATN-2 Leonard J. Gonerka, AX-3 James A. Lawrence. Shore batteries bracketed the helicopter during rescue. Ship closes to 12 miles off coast.
