## Logbook

While Mike was still at Treasure Island, the *San Diego Union* ran a front-page story on the USS Enterprise (CVN-65) arriving at North Island — the same carrier Mike and his buddy would visit. Page A-2 carried Vietnam War headlines: Buddhist opposition to Thieu, Chinese factional fighting in Canton, and U Thant reporting on Red Bloc aid to Hanoi.
