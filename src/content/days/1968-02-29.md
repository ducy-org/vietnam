## Logbook

At sea, SAR station. 1700 — GENERAL QUARTERS: MIG in area, 70 miles.

## Sources

> From Mike's letter, 21 Feb 1968: "No, as you probably have figured out by now Bill and I were unable to see each other in Da Nang. For obvious reasons that is not a liberty port... I figure that Bill and I are about 10,000 miles from home now that we are here." [Letter]

> From Mike's letter, 22 Feb 1968: "Tomorrow is payday. I will get $167. Thats tax-free base pay for two weeks and combat pay for 22 days." [Letter]

> From Mike's letter, 26 Feb 1968: "Over the weekend two planes did go down somewhere away from our station. They rescued the pilot from one with a helo from one of the carriers. The other ones they gave up for lost. There are three carriers operating in our vicinity. They are the Enterprise, Bon Homme Richard, and Ticonderoga. Watch for them in the news, of course they get all the publicity. The only time someone pays any attention to the other ships is when they get captured (the Pueblo)." [Letter]
