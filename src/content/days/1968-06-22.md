## Logbook

At sea, SAR/AAW station. 1630 — FIRE FIVE TERRIER MISSILES AT MIGS, RANGE 36 MILES. NO HITS CONFIRMED. Mail by helo — "1 tape, 1 letter." 2015 — Movie: "Assault on a Queen." Note: COR records "two salvoes of two and one Terrier HTR missiles at a flight of three MIG-21 aircraft" — Mike's logbook count of five may include the two from Jun 17 in a running total, or the COR may undercount.
