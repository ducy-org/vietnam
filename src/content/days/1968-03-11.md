## Logbook

At sea, SAR station. 1230 — SAR ALERT: Air Force F-4 Phantom reported downed 60 miles southwest, 300 yards inland. UNABLE TO FIND PILOTS. 1700 — Mail by helo.

## Sources

> From Mike's letter, 12 Mar 1968: "Yesterday an Air Force plane went down inland about 65 miles southwest of us. They made some attempts to find the pilots but gave them up for lost in a couple of hours." [Letter]
