## Logbook

Mike had been on mess cooking duty — 10-hour days on his feet preparing food, but preferred it to cleaning barracks. He reconnected with a buddy from Great Lakes boot camp who spotted Mike's name on a barracks list.

Mike noted the hippies sleeping in parks, painting on sidewalks, holding public speeches, walking around with kittens on strings.

Then the question that would lead to the Super 8 camera and the SAR rescue footage:

He also learned he'd passed the test for third class — *"I was very glad and surprized."*

## Sources

> "Sunday we went there and spent most of the day. We went to Fisherman's Wharf then went to the Haight/Ashbury section and paid a visit to the 'hippies.' There are a few of them downtown but if you want to see a lot of them you have to go there. I wish I had a camera with me that day." [Letter]

> "I think I am going to buy me a movie camera or do you think a still camera would be best. Just in case I do get one, what size projector do we have 8mm or 16mm? I'd like to take a lot of pictures to remember the places I've been and the things I've seen and will be seeing." [Letter]
