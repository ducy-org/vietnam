## Logbook

Underway for Pearl Harbor. 0800 — Arrive Pearl Harbor to load stores, fuel, and receive mail. "5 letters, 1 package (books), 1 movie film." 1230 — Liberty, base exchange, EM Club. 1900 — Underway for San Diego.

## Sources

> Meanwhile in Da Nang — Bill's letter, 13 July 1968: While Mike was at Pearl Harbor 5 days from home, Bill had 226 days left: "You should be back in the states by the time you get this letter you lucky fucker." Bill reacted to Mike's tape about the SAR rescues: "You said in your tape that the chopper got their ass's kicked when they went in land. Boy I bet those chopper guys were really shitting their skivvies when the V.C. started giving them hell." Two red alerts the previous week — rockets hit the air strip and Marble Mountain, 5 miles away. A jeep driver fell asleep at 4 AM and rolled 50 feet down an embankment. "Its been fairly quiet around here the past few nites, but we are expecting to be hit soon." Signed off with dark humor: "I keep you informed if anything big comes up. You know like pulling all the U.S. Troops out or something like that. ha ha!" [Bill's letter to Mike]
