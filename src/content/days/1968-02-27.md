## Logbook

At sea, SAR station. 1730 — GENERAL QUARTERS: MIG closes to 60 miles. No action taken. 2030 — Refuel from USS Tolovana (AO-64), 110,000 gallons. Mail brought aboard. 2200 — Replenish with USS Zelima (AF-49), 8 tons — "A JOUETT FIRST!"
