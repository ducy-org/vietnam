## Logbook

At sea, SAR station. 0550 — SAR ALERT: Plane downed 5 miles from Jouett. Recover debris — NO TRACE OF SURVIVORS. 1230 — Boat launched to search for classified materials thrown overboard. Likely the S-2E Tracker "Abilene 10" (BuNo 149274) from VS-23, USS Yorktown, lost the previous night (Mar 17) on a surveillance mission near Hon Me island. All four crew — CDR Donald R. Hubbs, LTJG Lee D. Benson, AX2 Randall J. Nightingale, and ADR3 Thomas D. Barber — remain unaccounted for to this day. Starboard wing section recovered Mar 20. "Shave off mustache."
