## Logbook

Mike saw Bill at Georgia Mae's over the weekend. The ship was undergoing final inspections and loading stores: 11,500 lbs of beef, 800 gallons of milk, 1,600 dozen eggs, and 800 lbs of popcorn. On January 9, Jouett loaded ammunition at Seal Beach Naval Weapons Station. [Familygram, 19 Jan 1968]

## Sources

> "I am glad I did come home during the holidays. I think it helped my morale a lot and I'll be able to face the months overseas a little better." [Letter]
