## Logbook

At sea, SAR station. 1215 — SAR ALERT: A-4E Skyhawk ("Champion-406") from VA-56 "Champions," USS Enterprise, downed 18.5 miles inland, 41 miles from Jouett. Location: 49mi 160° from Vinh, 19mi INLAND — the deepest inland rescue of the deployment. PILOT RESCUED by Big Mother (SH-3A Sea King, HC-7 Det 110). Crew: LT Gary D. McConnell (pilot), LTJG John W. Nichols (copilot), AX John R. Cullivan, AX-3 Alan K. Nobles. Returned to Jouett at 1315. 1600 — Intelligence briefing for crew.

## Sources

> Stars & Stripes, ~May 1968 — "2 Jets Lost in N. Viet": Mike annotated this clipping with the handwritten note "OUR HELO PICKED THEM UP" and starred the passage about the F-4 Phantom crew rescued near Vinh. The article reported 830 total American planes lost over North Vietnam. [Stars & Stripes]

> From Mike's letter, 8 May 1968 (typewritten): "This afternoon we rescued two more pilots who were shot down about 1/2 mile off the coast. We launched the helo and in about 30 minutes they were back with the pilots... We got with in 12 miles of the mainland which is the closest we have ever been up here. It was a real clear day and you could see a lot of the land over there... So, this makes four rescues for us and all of them in this line period." [Letter]

> From the May 19 Familygram: "In the most hazardous rescue of the three, JOUETT's helicopter had to fly deep into enemy territory and rescue the downed pilot from dense jungle." [Familygram]
