## Logbook

Mike's first letter from the Jouett, typed on USN stationery from the ship in Bremerton.

He was putting in for electronics correspondence courses to fill his evenings and saving money by staying aboard. Sent $80 home for the bank. The ship had gone to sea for weekend exercises — a boiler casualty brought them back early.

Looking ahead to summer 1968 leave — hoping to coordinate with Bill.
