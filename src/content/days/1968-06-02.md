## Logbook

Underway for Da Nang. 1200 — Anchor in Da Nang Harbor. 1400–1600 — Stand .50 cal machine gun watch. 1750 — Underway for South SAR station. Conduct PT boat exercises.

## Sources

> From Mike's letter, 28 May 1968: "I got a tape from Bill yesterday. His letters never say much but he sure had a lot to talk about on the tape. I'll send him one back tonight." [Letter]

> From Bill's note, 27 May 1968: A brief cover letter with pictures and tape recorder specs: "Here the pictures and the speed and type of recorder — '3 3/4 I.P.S.' 'MONO.' Be looking for a tape from you real soon too." Signed: "Little Brother, Bill USMC." The brothers were exchanging tapes between the Tonkin Gulf and Hoi An — reel-to-reel at 3¾ ips, mono. [Bill's letter to Mike]
