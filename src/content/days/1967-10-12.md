## Logbook

Still in Bremerton, preparing to transit to San Diego. The Cardinals won the World Series. Mike had been aboard two weeks and was settling in — eight FTs and eight gunner's mates in his division.

He was chasing his third-class advancement — the reserve center still hadn't entered it in his record. His leading petty officer and the ship's office were writing to the reserve center. Mike sent $50 home for the bank.

Bill sent $5 of the money he owed — *"That came as a surprise to me."*

## Sources

> "I don't think that things could be much better than what they are here on the ship. The guys in the division are really good to work with and they think that I will have no trouble in getting acquainted with the operation of some of the equipment that we use." [Letter]
