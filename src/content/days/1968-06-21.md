At sea, SAR/AAW station. Holiday routine.

## Sources

> From the Jul 1 Familygram: "On 21 June, JOUETT was paid a great honor and enjoyed a rare privilege when she was visited by three of the most important U.S. Navy Commanders in the Pacific Fleet... Admiral John J. HYLAND, USN; Vice Admiral William F. BRINGLE, USN; and Rear Admiral Ralph W. COUSINS, USN... He predicted that in years to come they would be happy and proud to know that they had served their nation when it needed them." [Familygram]

> Meanwhile in Da Nang — Bill's letter, 21 June 1968: While three admirals were visiting Jouett to congratulate the crew on the Medal of Honor rescue, Bill was on mess duty: "Sounds as if you poor squids had a little action up your way. Just make sure you get those MIG pilots back safe and fuck the others (ha ha.)" He'd seen Bob White from back home, now in the Seabees in Hoi An. Shopping for a Sony 907 tape recorder from an Allied catalog ($34.50) — PX had one for $31 but 3–4 month wait: "So I said fuck that!" Enclosed the $30 he owed Mike. Signed off: "Be good and don't 'fall off the ship.'" [Bill's letter to Mike; water-damaged scan, fully legible]
