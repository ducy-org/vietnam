## Logbook

At sea, SAR station. Cloudy, rainy, cool. 2030 — Refuel from USS Neches (AO-47).

## Sources

> From Mike's letter, 18 Feb 1968 — FIRST LETTER FROM THE COMBAT ZONE: "While we were anchored there I had an M-1 rifle and a clip of cartridges by my side during the day while I was topside working. When they say there are about 500 air strikes a day you better believe it. With all the air traffic a person might think he was at O'Hare or some other large airport. We could hear some shooting in the hills but we were in a safe place (I think)." [Letter]

> From Mike's letter, 18 Feb 1968: "I have been keeping a day to day log of the days on the cruise. By the time we get back to the states I'll have a good summary of what and where we went." — This is the logbook that forms the backbone of this timeline. [Letter]

> From Mike's letter, 18 Feb 1968: "Everyone looks at this from a serious view and thats the way it should be. I often wonder about Bill, knowing he is close around here somewhere. I know he is going to have some hard times in the year he is over here and I also pray that he can make it okay. As for myself I can think of no better way and safer way to get in the combat duty and extra $65 a month than to be right here where I'm at." [Letter]

> From the Feb 18 Familygram: "Since that morning when we cast off all lines and slowly moved away from Pier Two in San Diego, we have cruised almost 8,000 miles." [Familygram]
