## Logbook

At sea, SAR station. 2100 — Officially relieved on South SAR by USS Horne (DLG-30). Set course for Subic Bay, P.I.

## Sources

> From Mike's letter, 29 Jun 1968 — LAST LETTER FROM THE COMBAT ZONE: "It's about 12:30 AM Saturday morning here and I'm on watch... We get relieved up here at 8:00 AM Sunday then head for Subic Bay." [Letter]

> From Mike's letter, 29 Jun 1968: "Since I made the last tape on the 23rd, there hasn't been anything new happen up here. We had our last refueling here Wednesday night. Of course every day they have MIG tracking alerts several times but they seldom get within 40 miles from us." [Letter]

> From Mike's letter, 29 Jun 1968: "I just remembered this will be the last 'free' letter I write from over here. Boy, thats too bad! We are starting to lose out on some of the combat zone benefits now that it's time to leave." [Letter]
