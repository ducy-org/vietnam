## Sources

This was two months after the assassination of Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. The Jun 20 Jouett Jargon carried a story about the resulting Presidential Commission on Violence chaired by Milton Eisenhower, which described investigating "political assassination, criminal attacks on individuals and civil disorders such as riots."
