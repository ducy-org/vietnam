## Logbook

At sea, SAR station. 1500 — Boat from USS Sutherland (DD-743) brings remains and debris of A-6A Intruder (BuNo 152944) from VA-35, USS Enterprise, to Jouett. The aircraft was lost on a night strike northeast of Haiphong. Crew: LCDR Thomas E. Scheurich (pilot, Nebraska) and LTJG Richard C. "Tito" Lannom (bombardier/navigator, Union City, TN) — both KIA. Their remains were not identified for over 50 years: Lannom in 2018, Scheurich in 2025. Both eventually laid to rest with full military honors. 1630 — Mail by helo. 2000 — Refuel from USS Taluga (AO-).
