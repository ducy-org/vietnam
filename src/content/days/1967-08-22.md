## Logbook

Mike spent his first days at Treasure Island in classification, physicals, and shots. He picked Long Beach and San Diego for home ports, Hawaii for overseas. With little to do between assignments, he explored San Francisco — ballgames, Chinatown, Fisherman's Wharf, cable cars, movies.

He asked his parents to check with the reserve center about his third-class advancement test results. Temperature in San Francisco: 60s, wearing blues.
