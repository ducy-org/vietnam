## Logbook

Underway for Guam. 0830 — Clear San Bernardino Straits. Observe active volcano in straits. 2000 — "Moonlighting as an SK, a job I 'volunteered' for. Hours 2000–0600." Inventory ship's storeroom.

## Sources

> From the Jul 5 Familygram: "Since departing Tonkin Gulf on 1 July, the JOLLY 'J' has been traveling fast on our homeward voyage... Across the almost glassy calm of the blue Philippine Sea, JOUETT left a beautiful wake that seemed to stretch all the way to the horizon. Sunbathing during the noon meal hours became a favorite pastime." [Familygram]
