## Logbook

In port Subic Bay. Duty.

## Sources

> From Mike's letter, 26 Mar 1968: "After the 2½ months of this cruise I am well convinced this isn't the life for me. Some of the nonsense and fools you have to put up really get under your skin during these days at sea." [Letter]

> From Mike's letter, 26 Mar 1968: "Since I have last wrote we have had two fires here on the ship. One was in a radar power room and yesterday a transformer burnt up... Things like that can sink a ship just as well as anything else." [Letter]

> From Mike's letter, 26 Mar 1968: "We left the Gulf of Tonkin Friday evening after 34 days up there." [Letter]

> From the Apr 8 Familygram: "Vietnam, as you may know, was under the influence of the northeast monsoon during JOUETT's first period on station; rain, fog, and low visibility were standard during the five weeks we spent in Tonkin Gulf." [Familygram]
